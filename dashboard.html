Section 01
🧠 What Is This Indicator?
CONCEPT OVERVIEW

This is a multi-confluence institutional trading indicator built on Smart Money Concepts (SMC) combined with ICT Kill Zone timing. It tracks what institutional traders (banks, hedge funds, market makers) do on the chart and alerts you to enter after they've shown their hand — not before. You are reacting to institutional footprints, not predicting price.

CORE PHILOSOPHY

Institutions cannot hide their movements. They leave 3 clues on every chart: Liquidity sweeps (stop hunts), Order blocks (zones of interest), and Fair Value Gaps (imbalances they need to fill). This indicator detects all 3 simultaneously — and only fires a signal when they align inside a Kill Zone window.

Section 05
🎯 Step-by-Step Trade Execution
1
CHECK DASHBOARD BEFORE SESSION OPENS

Open TradingView 5 minutes before Kill Zone starts. Check the dashboard: What is HTF Bias? Where is Asian High/Low? Is there an open FVG or OB nearby? Build your mental map before price starts moving.

2
WAIT FOR THE 💧 SWEEP LABEL

Do nothing until a Sweep label appears on the chart. If it says "SWEEP (Sellside)" — institutions hunted longs below, reversal up expected. If it says "SWEEP (Buyside)" — institutions hunted stops above, reversal down expected. This is your trigger event.

3
CONFIRM THE SIGNAL GRADE IN DASHBOARD

Immediately look at the Dashboard → Signal Grade field. If it says ⭐ A-GRADE — proceed to entry. If it says B-Grade, reduce size or skip. If it says Waiting — sit on your hands. Grade determines sizing, not emotion.

4
FIND THE ORDER BLOCK ON 15M CHART

Look for the cyan (Bull OB) or red (Bear OB) box near the sweep zone. Wait for price to pull back INTO this box. The 50% level of the OB box is your ideal limit order entry. If OB has an FVG inside it — that's your exact entry zone.

5
WAIT FOR CHoCH CONFIRMATION

The CHoCH label must appear on the 5M or 15M chart after price taps the OB. A CHoCH ▲ = buyer confirmation for longs. A CHoCH ▼ = seller confirmation for shorts. No CHoCH = no entry. This single rule eliminates most false signals.

6
EXECUTE WITH PROPER SL & 3 TPs

Enter at the OB 50% level or on CHoCH candle close. Place SL beyond the sweep wick. Use 3 targets:

Section 06
🛡️ Non-Negotiable Risk Rules
  • Max risk per trade: 1–2% of total account only
  • Minimum Risk-to-Reward before entry: 2:1 minimum — if you can't get 2R, skip the trade
  • Max trades per Kill Zone session: 2 trades
  • Max trades per full day: 3 trades total
  • If 2 stop losses hit in same day: Stop trading, close platform, come back tomorrow
  • Always check economic calendar before London and NY opens — news events override all technical setups
  • Never move SL further from entry to avoid being stopped out — most dangerous habit in trading
  • After TP1 is hit, always move SL to breakeven. A free trade running = no stress trading

Section 01
📋 Every Row Explained
⏰
ROW 1 — KILL ZONE
Which trading session is currently active?

This row tells you whether it is the right time to trade or not. Only trade during 3 sessions — everything outside these windows is noise. The indicator auto-detects the active session and also changes the chart background color accordingly.

🌙 Asian KZ — Range Build Only. Do not trade.
🇬🇧 London KZ — Best session. Trend set here.
🇺🇸 NY KZ — Power move session.
⚫ No KZ — Outside all windows. Do not trade.
📈
ROW 2 — HTF BIAS
What is the overall direction on the 4H chart?

HTF = Higher Time Frame. This row reads the market structure from the 4H chart and determines whether the trend is up or down. If the bias is BULLISH, only take long trades. If BEARISH, only take short trades. Never trade against the HTF Bias — trading against structure destroys accounts.

📈 BULLISH — Only take LONG trades
📉 BEARISH — Only take SHORT trades
💧
ROW 3 — LAST SWEEP
Which liquidity grab did institutions just execute?

A liquidity sweep happens when price briefly breaks a recent high or low — triggering retail stop losses — and then immediately reverses back. This is your trigger event. Only consider a trade after a sweep has occurred.

💧 Sellside — Price broke below a low and recovered → Look for LONG
💧 Buyside — Price broke above a high and rejected → Look for SHORT
— No sweep yet — Wait patiently
⚠️

Your screen showed: "Buyside" sweep + BULLISH bias — this is a conflict. That is exactly why Signal Grade showed "Waiting." The indicator was protecting you — perfectly correct behavior.

📊
ROW 4 — FVG (Fair Value Gap)
Is there an imbalance zone present near the sweep?

A Fair Value Gap forms when price moves so aggressively that a gap is left between 3 consecutive candles. When an FVG aligns with an Order Block, that is your exact entry zone.

✅ Bull FVG — Bullish imbalance — Long entry zone
✅ Bear FVG — Bearish imbalance — Short entry zone
— None — No FVG. A-Grade signal not possible.
🌙
ROW 5 — ASIAN H/L
Previous Asian session range — your liquidity targets

These two numbers show the High and Low of the previous Asian session — the levels where retail stop losses cluster. During London and NY sessions, price commonly sweeps these levels first before reversing. Use them as your TP2 targets.

📍 Top number = Asian High (buyside liquidity pool)
📍 Bottom number = Asian Low (sellside liquidity pool)

Section 03
🗺️ From "Waiting" to "GO LONG" — Exact Journey
STEP 01
Kill Zone Becomes Active
Dashboard Kill Zone row shows London KZ or NY KZ. Chart background color changes. You are now in alert mode.
STEP 02
HTF Bias Is Clear
Row 2 clearly shows BULLISH or BEARISH. If the market is ranging or unclear, do not trade this session.
STEP 03
💧 Sweep Label Appears on Chart
A "SWEEP" label fires on the chart. The Last Sweep row in the dashboard updates. This is your trigger event.
STEP 04
Sweep Direction Matches HTF Bias
BULLISH bias requires SELLSIDE sweep → long reversal. BEARISH bias requires BUYSIDE sweep → short reversal. Mismatch = "Waiting" stays. Wait for correct alignment.
STEP 05
FVG Appears Near the Sweep
Dashboard shows "Bull FVG" or "Bear FVG." Signal Grade upgrades to A-GRADE automatically.
🔱 FINAL STEP
Action Row → "GO LONG (A)" or "GO SHORT (A)"
All 4 conditions aligned. Go to 15M chart, find the Order Block, wait for CHoCH confirmation, and execute your entry.
